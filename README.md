# Sales Analysis with Python

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to understand product and regional performance, profitability, sales volume, and product-region opportunities.

## Tools

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

## Analysis

* Product-level sales and profit analysis
* Regional sales and profit analysis
* Profit margin analysis
* Profit per unit analysis
* Product-region performance comparison
* Performance classification using quantity and profit-margin benchmarks
* Data visualization

## Key Findings

* **West** generated the highest overall sales and profit among the four regions.
* **Monitor — West** generated the highest product-region profit.
* **Camera — South** was another strong product-region performer.
* **Tablet** showed relatively weak profitability across regions.
* **Printer — North** showed particularly weak performance, with both relatively low quantity and profit margin.
* Product performance varies by region, showing that the best product overall may not be the best product in every region.

## Business Recommendations

* Protect and maintain strong product-region combinations such as Monitor in the West and Camera in the South.
* Investigate high-volume, low-margin products to identify opportunities for improving profitability.
* Review consistently weaker products such as Tablet to understand pricing, demand, and cost issues.
* Investigate regional underperformance, particularly Printer in the North.
* Use product-region analysis rather than relying only on overall product performance when making business decisions.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that overall sales and profit do not tell the complete story. Product performance varies considerably by region, making product-region analysis useful for identifying strong performers, growth opportunities, and areas requiring improvement.
